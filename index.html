A little math, a little music

Make a function
sound like something.

Type a formula or upload a clear picture of one. We’ll plot it, then turn its values into changing notes—right in your browser.

1. Enter a function

Try sin(x), x^2, or 2*sin(3*x).

Supported: + − × ÷, powers (^), parentheses, x, pi, e, and sin, cos, tan, sqrt, abs, log, exp. OCR is a first draft—check the recognized formula before plotting.

2. Your curve

y = sin(x)

3. Hear it

As x moves across the range, function height controls pitch. Values are safely mapped into audible notes.

Audio and graph processing happen locally in your browser. Image text recognition uses the Tesseract.js library loaded from its public CDN.
